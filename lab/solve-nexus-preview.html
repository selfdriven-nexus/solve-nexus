How it solves

One loop, at every scale.

A household budget, a product launch, a city's housing plan — every problem is worked through the same five-step cycle. Each pass draws on verified context and writes what it learned back into it.

  1. 01 · Observe

    See the verified state

    Start from what is actually true now — people, assets, commitments, evidence and history — read straight from the verified context graph, not reconstructed from documents.

  2. 02 · Interpret

    Separate proof from assumption

    Deterministic reasoning resolves policy, authority and provenance, and marks what is proven apart from what is assumed — before anyone forms an opinion.

  3. 03 · Decide

    Choose, with a conductor

    Options are generated and explained in plain language. A named human conductor weighs them and makes the call — on the record.

  4. 04 · Act

    Coordinate the work

    People and delegated AI agents act under scoped, time-limited, revocable authority, so every action traces back to the decision that allowed it.

  5. 05 · Learn

    Write it back

    Outcomes, evidence and reflections return to the graph as living memory. The next problem starts where this one finished.

↺ Then observe again — every completion makes the next cycle sharper.

The AI Trinity

Truth flows toward language.

Today's AI tries to remember, reason and speak with one statistical model. solve.nexus separates them — the architecture set out on selfdriven.nexus — so every answer is grounded in verified state before it is ever put into words.

Read from the ground up: the graph holds what is true, the processor proves what follows from it, and the language model explains, plans and creates on top of it.

  • L1 · The nervous system

    Verified Context GraphVCG

    Persistent, verifiable memory of every person, asset, policy, event and decision — with identity, relationships, evidence and provenance attached.

  • L2 · The cognitive engine

    Verified Context ProcessorVCP

    Deterministic reasoning over verified context — resolving authority, policy, provenance and trust as they change, with every step explainable.

  • L3 · The voice

    Large Language ModelLLM

    Communication, creativity and planning on top of state it can rely on — as fluent as ever, and finally answerable to evidence.

Human conductors

AI handles the volume. People hold the judgment.

Every problem has a human conductor who orchestrates the work — setting direction, weighing trade-offs, owning the relationships and the ethics. AI agents act under authority delegated from that person, anchored in KERI identifiers and ACDC credentials.

  • Conductors

    Strategy, judgment, relationships, ethics and accountability — the calls only a person should make.

  • Agents

    Volume, consistency, monitoring, reporting and automation — tireless, and always on a short leash.

  • Delegationdip

    Agents hold delegated identifiers with scoped, time-limited, revocable authority, so every action resolves to the human who authorised it.

  • Conducting scores

    Each problem's score records outcomes, assumptions, risks, decisions, evidence and reflections — living memory, not paperwork.
